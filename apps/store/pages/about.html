Your Local Halal Shop in E12

Pakeeza Hilal Meat is a family-run shop on High Street North, E12 6SB, proudly serving the local community with fresh halal meat, poultry, grocery essentials and frozen foods - all in one convenient place.

Every piece of meat we sell is hand-slaughtered by a trained Muslim slaughterman following strict Islamic guidelines and certified by a recognised UK Halal authority. We source directly from trusted UK farms, ensuring the highest standards of freshness and quality.

Whether you need fresh chicken, lamb, beef, grocery items or frozen foods - we've got you covered. We also offer free local delivery on all orders over -30 to E12 and surrounding postcodes. Call us on 020 8503 5721.

Our Commitment

What We Stand For

Halal Integrity

We never compromise on halal standards. Every animal is hand-slaughtered by a trained Muslim slaughterman, blessed with Bismillah, and certified by a recognised Halal authority.

Freshness First

We receive fresh deliveries daily from trusted UK farms. We never sell frozen meat as fresh - everything is prepared the same day for maximum quality and taste.

Community First

We're proud members of the East Ham community. We support local events, provide special Eid packages and always go the extra mile for our loyal customers.

Certified Halal

Our Halal Certification

All of our meat products carry the official Halal certification from a recognised UK Halal authority. Our certification covers the entire supply chain - from farm to shop counter.

Our certifying body conducts regular audits to ensure our practices meet the highest halal standards. We are fully transparent about our processes and welcome any questions.
